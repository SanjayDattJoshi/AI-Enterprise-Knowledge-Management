Section 4: Medical Coverage Options

The company offers three distinct medical plans for the 2024 plan year, designed to provide comprehensive coverage while allowing employees to choose the option that best fits their personal and family needs. All plans include preventative care covered at 100% when using in-network providers.

The PPO (Preferred Provider Organization) plan offers the most flexibility, allowing employees to see any provider, though out-of-pocket costs are significantly lower when utilizing the preferred network.

"For the 2024 calendar year, the employer contribution to the Health Savings Account (HSA) paired with the High Deductible Health Plan (HDHP) will increase to $1,000 for individual coverage and $2,000 for family coverage. These funds are deposited in bi-weekly installments starting with the first payroll in January."

Employees must elect their HSA contribution amount during the open enrollment period. Contributions can be adjusted mid-year if a qualifying life event occurs. Note that total contributions (employer + employee) cannot exceed the IRS annual limits.
