How many annual leave days do employees receive?

auto_awesome
AI Generated Answer

Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year1. This is prorated for part-time staff based on their contracted hours.

In addition to the standard 20 days, employees gain one additional day of annual leave for every two years of continuous service, up to a maximum of 25 total days2. Leave must be requested at least two weeks in advance for periods longer than three days.
